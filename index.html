03 — Research Projects

Robust Music Genre Classification under Noisy Mashup Conditions

Built for the Messy Mashup competition, evaluating genre-classification robustness under realistic, noisy mixing conditions — tempo-synced, same-genre stem mashups with randomized noise — across 10 genres and 4 instrument stems (drums, vocals, bass, other). Converted stems to spectrograms and fine-tuned AST and HuBERT alongside a custom CNN with a Squeeze-and-Excitation attention mechanism, reaching 92% accuracy on the noisy mashup test set.

Python · PyTorch · scikit-learn · matplotlib · seaborn

Comment Category Prediction

Built for the Comment Category Prediction Challenge: predicted the final category assigned to user comments from interaction feedback, symbolic expressions, topic-reference indicators, and system signals across textual, numerical, and categorical features. Engineered features across all three modalities and compared Logistic Regression, LightGBM, and XGBoost with hyperparameter tuning, reaching 82% accuracy.

Python · scikit-learn · LightGBM · XGBoost
